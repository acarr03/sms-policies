Privacy Policy

Adrian Carrera Personal Assistant · Last updated October 8, 2026

Adrian Carrera Personal Assistant ("we," "us") is a personal text messaging service run by Adrian Carrera. It sends reminders, to-do lists, and schedule notifications by text message to Adrian Carrera and to friends and family who have agreed to receive them.

Information we collect

Your mobile phone number, your opt-in and opt-out status, and the text messages exchanged with our number.

How we use it

Only to send you the reminders and notifications you agreed to receive, to respond to HELP requests, and to honor STOP requests.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your phone number is shared only with our text messaging provider (Twilio), and only to deliver messages to you.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.

How to sign up

Adrian Carrera personally invites friends and family and shares the number. To sign up, text START to (909) 858-4354, then reply Y to confirm. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
