Privacy Policy

Adrian Carrera Notifications · Last updated October 8, 2026

Adrian Carrera Notifications ("we," "us") is an automated text notification service operated by Adrian Carrera, a sole proprietor. It sends reminders, task lists, and schedule notifications by text message to recipients who have agreed to receive them.

Information we collect

Your mobile phone number, your opt-in and opt-out status, and the text messages exchanged with our number.

How we use it

Only to send you the reminders and notifications you agreed to receive, to respond to HELP requests, and to honor STOP requests.

Sharing

Mobile phone numbers will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your phone number is shared only with our text messaging provider (Twilio), and only to deliver messages to you.

Message frequency and costs

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.

How to sign up

The number is provided directly to people who request notifications. No one is enrolled without opting in. To sign up, text START to (909) 858-4354, then reply Y to confirm. You are enrolled only after you reply Y. See the sign-up details. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
